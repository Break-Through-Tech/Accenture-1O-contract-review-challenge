# Selected CUAD category definitions

These are the ten CUAD categories selected by the team. Category membership describes what a provision says; it is not itself a risk rating.

| `category_id` | CUAD category | Extraction scope |
|---|---|---|
| `governing_law` | Governing Law | The state, province, country, or other jurisdiction whose law governs interpretation of the contract. |
| `renewal_term` | Renewal Term | The renewal period after the initial term, including automatic and unilateral extensions. |
| `revenue_profit_sharing` | Revenue/Profit Sharing | A requirement that one party share revenue or profit with the counterparty for covered technology, goods, or services. |
| `cap_on_liability` | Cap on Liability | A maximum amount, recovery limit, or claim-time limit applied to liability for breach. |
| `uncapped_liability` | Uncapped Liability | Liability that is unlimited generally or for a stated type of breach, such as IP infringement or confidentiality breach. |
| `termination_for_convenience` | Termination for Convenience | A right to terminate without cause by providing notice and allowing any waiting period to expire. |
| `anti_assignment` | Anti-Assignment | A requirement for consent or notice when a party assigns the contract to a third party. |
| `audit_rights` | Audit Rights | A right to inspect books, records, or locations to verify contractual compliance. |
| `license_grant` | License Grant | A license granted by one contracting party to the other. |
| `exclusivity` | Exclusivity | An exclusive dealing commitment, requirements obligation, restriction on third-party licensing or sales, or restriction on collaboration with others. |

## Linked review bundles

- Cap on Liability + Uncapped Liability
- License Grant + Exclusivity
- Renewal Term + Termination for Convenience
- Revenue/Profit Sharing + Audit Rights
- Governing Law + Anti-Assignment

Linked categories retain separate CUAD labels while sharing review context and, where appropriate, an `issue_family_id` for contract rollup.
